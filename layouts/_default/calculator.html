Pro Paracord Tips from Jacob

📏 Measure Snug, Not Tight:

Wrap a piece of string or tape around your wrist bone without slack. Mark where it overlaps and measure flat against a ruler.

🔥 The 1 Foot Rule:

For standard Cobra weaves, the classic formula is 1 foot of cord per 1 inch of wrist size, plus 1 foot extra for finishing ends.

✂️ Pre-shrinking Paracord:

Commercial 550 cord can shrink 5-10% when wet. If you plan to wear it swimming or in the shower, soak your cord in hot water before weaving!
